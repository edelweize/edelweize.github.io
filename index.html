About

About Me

My career started at the service desk of computer stores in Manado, where I spent six years deploying, repairing, and maintaining computers for everyday users. After earning my degree in Informatics Engineering, I moved into internet installation and ISP network work, then into website maintenance, SEO, and document control for construction projects.

These roles share one thread: keeping technology running and making sure the work is documented. I am comfortable talking to non-technical users, working in the field with network equipment, maintaining a website, and writing the report that explains what was done.

I am based in Manado, Indonesia (UTC+8) and work with clients and teams remotely.
