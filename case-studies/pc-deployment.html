IT Support · 2009 – 2015

PC Deployment & System Maintenance

Six years of hands-on support at two computer stores in Manado, serving customers in the store and at their own locations.

Project Overview

At Harvest Computer (2009–2011) and Blessing Computer (2011–2015), I worked as an IT Support Technician. Customers were a mix of individuals, offices, and schools. I worked both at the store counter and on site at customer locations.

Problem or Challenge

Customers brought in a wide range of problems: desktop PCs and laptops with hardware faults, Windows installations that needed repair or a clean reinstall, printers that had stopped working, and small offices that needed their computers connected on a local network. Most customers were not technical and depended on the device for daily work.

My Role

  • Diagnosed and repaired hardware faults in desktop PCs and laptops.
  • Repaired and reinstalled Windows, and installed Linux when required.
  • Installed and configured software, from office applications to entertainment software.
  • Repaired and set up printers.
  • Set up small local area networks (LAN).
  • Deployed new PCs and laptops, ready for use.

Technical Approach

Each job started with the customer's description of the problem, followed by checking whether the cause was in the hardware or the software. From there the fix was a repair, a replacement part, or a clean operating system installation, and the device was tested before it went back to the customer.

Tools and Technologies

  • Windows
  • Linux
  • Desktop & Laptop Hardware
  • Printers
  • LAN Setup
  • Office Software

Results or Operational Benefits

Customers received working, tested devices. Offices and schools had new computers installed and configured for them, and small offices had their computers connected on a local network.

Lessons Learned

The lesson that stayed with me most is the value of regular maintenance. Many of the faults customers brought in could have been avoided with routine care of the computer or laptop.
