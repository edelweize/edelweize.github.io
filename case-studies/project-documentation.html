Documentation · 2020 – 2021, 2023 – 2024

Project Documentation & Reporting

Document control and reporting for two conveyor construction projects at PT Surya Mulia Sampurna.

Project Overview

I worked as Document Control on two construction projects for PT Surya Mulia Sampurna: the HOPE Project (2020–2021), which built a conveyor system for a warehouse, and the TUNA Project (2023–2024), which built a conveyor system for a jetty. The client was PT Cargill Indonesia (CCP Amurang).

Problem or Challenge

A construction project produces documents every day: work progress, safety activities, and incidents on site. The client needed regular, reliable reports, and every document sent or received had to follow the document control procedure.

My Role

  • Created, edited, and archived project documents.
  • Followed document control procedures for transmitting and receiving documents.
  • Documented work progress and prepared daily and monthly progress reports.
  • Documented safety activities, including meetings, training, and inspections.
  • Prepared safety reports, including reports on incidents that happened on site.
  • Created and maintained the project timeline.
  • Sent reports to the client, PT Cargill Indonesia.

Technical Approach

Progress and safety activities were recorded as they happened and compiled into daily reports. Those fed the monthly progress and safety reports sent to the client. Project timelines were built and updated in Microsoft Excel, and finished documents were archived as PDF.

Tools and Technologies

  • Microsoft Excel
  • Microsoft Word
  • Adobe Acrobat

Results or Operational Benefits

The client received daily, monthly, and safety reports throughout both projects. Project documents were archived and traceable, and the timeline gave a current view of progress.

Lessons Learned

Consistent daily records make the monthly report straightforward to prepare. Careful documentation is the same discipline I apply to technical work.
