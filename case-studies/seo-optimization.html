SEO · 2022 – 2023

SEO & Website Optimization

Applying keyword research and on-page SEO to a confidential client's websites, in WordPress and in plain HTML.

Project Overview

Alongside website maintenance, I handled search engine optimization for the same client's websites. The goal was to help the pages appear for relevant searches on Google. The client's name is withheld for confidentiality.

Problem or Challenge

A website that search engines cannot understand well is hard for customers to find. The pages needed clear titles, descriptions, and content matched to the words people actually search for.

My Role

  • Carried out keyword research.
  • Wrote and managed page titles and meta tags.
  • Applied on-page SEO in WordPress using Yoast SEO.
  • Applied SEO techniques directly in the HTML of non-WordPress pages.
  • Monitored search performance with Google Search Console.
  • Worked on link building and general website optimization.

Technical Approach

I used Ahrefs for keyword research as a reference, not as the only measure. Chosen keywords were then applied to page titles, meta tags, and content. WordPress pages were optimized with Yoast SEO, plain HTML pages were optimized by hand, and Google Search Console was used to watch how pages performed in search.

Tools and Technologies

  • Google Search Console
  • Yoast SEO
  • Ahrefs
  • WordPress
  • HTML Meta Tags
  • Keyword Research

Results or Operational Benefits

Results varied. After optimization, some pages moved up in Google search results and others did not. No ranking figures were recorded, so none are claimed here. What the work did deliver was pages with consistent titles, meta tags, and keyword targeting.

Lessons Learned

SEO techniques applied directly in HTML are the foundation that tools build on. I also learned that optimization improves a page's chances but does not guarantee a ranking.
